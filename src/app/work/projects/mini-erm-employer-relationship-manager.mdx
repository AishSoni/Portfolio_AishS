## Overview

Mini ERM centralizes job applications and outreach for a user. Applications, contacts, resumes, events, and notifications are user-scoped, while shared identifiers connect the records into one relationship and follow-up workflow.

## What it does

- Accepts a job link through Telegram, generates LinkedIn and Apollo search URLs, captures recruiter contacts, creates the application, attaches a resume, and triggers an n8n cold-email workflow.
- Serves tracked resume pages that record views and classify likely recruiter activity versus automated scanners using timing, IP ranges, user-agent patterns, dwell time, and scroll depth.
- Runs application lifecycle rules for deduplication by company and role, reactivation, expiry, status changes, follow-up cadence, response-based cancellation, and idempotency keys.
- Includes scheduled status and notification jobs plus a Next.js resume surface and analytics dashboard.

## Design focus

The backend is designed as a user-scoped multi-tenant service with Supabase Storage and PostgreSQL. Shared Zod schemas keep API boundaries explicit across the web app, Telegram bot, n8n workflows, and scheduled jobs.

## Stack

TypeScript, Node.js, Express, Zod, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Next.js, n8n, Telegram, and scheduled background jobs.
